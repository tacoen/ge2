prepareForm / handleForm

Every layout below is built from utils.css classes — .flex, .column, .middle, .w* and the -md variants — plus three page-layer grouping classes that space by margin, never gap: .stack (vertical), .cluster (inline) and .cluster-md (stacked on phones, inline from md). A bare <label> stacks its text above the field; add .flex.middle to put them side by side.
